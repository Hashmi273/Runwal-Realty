Frequently Asked Questions

What is the relationship between Runwal Realty and the erstwhile Runwal Group?

+

From 2016 onwards, the erstwhile Runwal Group has operated as two independent entities — Runwal Realty, led by Mr. Sandeep Runwal, and Runwal Enterprises, led by Mr. Subodh Runwal — each with its own distinct leadership, identity, and strategic direction.

Founded by Mr. Subhash Runwal in 1978, the Runwal name has been built on a legacy of trust, quality, design excellence, and a deeply customer-first philosophy. Today, that legacy continues independently through both companies.

What are the key residential projects by Runwal Realty?

+

Runwal Realty is renowned for crafting luxury high-rise residences and landmark mixed-use developments. Key residential developments include Runwal Malabar at Malabar Hill, Runwal Raaya in Worli, Runwal Auris in Malad, Runwal One in Thane, Runwal Lands End in Kolshet, Runwal Timeless in Wadala, Runwal Rare in Andheri West, and Runwal The Central Park in PCMC Pune.

Does Runwal Realty develop commercial and retail properties?

+

Yes. Runwal Realty has an extensive commercial and retail footprint including the iconic R City Mall in Ghatkopar West (spanning over 1.2 million square feet with 350+ global brands), R Mall Thane, R Mall Mulund, R Odeon, as well as premium IGBC-certified commercial office parks like R Square in Andheri East and Mulund.

Runwal Realty - Crafting Addresses Across Mumbai, Thane & Pune Since 1978

Runwal Realty, the brand of Runwal Developers Ltd., has been developing residential, commercial, and retail real estate across Mumbai, Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), and Pune since 1978. Founded by Subhash Runwal and led today by Promoter & Managing Director Sandeep Runwal, the company has delivered 35 completed projects spanning 11.22 million square feet, building with precision and long-term value at every scale.

Today, Runwal Realty has 17 ongoing developments across Mumbai, Thane, and Pune. The residential portfolio spans ultra-luxury sky mansions at Malabar Hill, sea-view residences in Worli and Parel, integrated township living in Thane, and a 26-acre mixed-use development in Chinchwad, Pune.

On the commercial side, R Square in Andheri and Mulund offer IGBC-certified office addresses for modern businesses. The retail portfolio includes R City in Ghatkopar West (1.2 million sq ft housing 350+ global brands), R Mall, and R Odeon. The commitment that has guided every project remains unchanged: building for generations to come.

All figures sourced from the Runwal Developers Ltd. Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filed with SEBI, September 2025.

Runwal Group Transitions into Two Independent Groups — Runwal Realty and Runwal Enterprises

From 2016 onwards, the Group has operated as two distinct entities — Runwal Realty and Runwal Enterprises — each with its own leadership, identity, and strategic direction.

For nearly five decades, the Runwal name has stood for integrity, quality, and a deeply customer-first philosophy. Founded by Mr. Subhash Runwal in 1978, the Group built its legacy on trust, design excellence, and the belief that every family deserves a home designed to last for generations.

Runwal Realty, led by Mr. Sandeep Runwal, has emerged as one of India's leading luxury and lifestyle real estate developers, with over 35 delivered projects across MMR and Pune.
